FIELD MANUAL

How to play.

Golden scout: Tap or click a destination to move. Tap near a food clump to collect it; return to the black nest to deposit.
Orders: Forage sends escorts to food, Rally recruits nearby workers, Attack encourages soldier combat, and Defend guards the queen.
More ants: The queen automatically lays eggs when enough food is stored. Eggs hatch into workers and soldiers.
Fight: Move your scout or rallied soldiers close to red ants. Destroy the red queen to claim the patch.
Backyard: After winning a tile, open Backyard and tap an adjacent frontier tile to expand.
View controls: Drag with two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom, or use + / −. A single tap sets your scout's destination. Mouse wheel zooms; arrow keys move your scout.
