MISSION 01 · FIRST LIGHT

One queen.
One chance to thrive.

You're starting from the beginning: just four workers, a scout and a tiny burrow. Create a living colony before the rival red ants discover you.

01 — Feed. Choose Workers and send them to nearby seeds. They carry food underground into the granary.
02 — Dig. In the nest, mark soil to excavate. Workers carve tunnels and construct a nursery.
03 — Defend and conquer. Train soldiers, hold the border, survive a raid and take the enemy outpost.

Five real objectives · Persistent progress · Touch and mouse controls

FIELD GUIDE

Command the
whole colony.

Select: Tap one ant or use the scout, worker, soldier or all-ants group buttons. On desktop, Shift-drag selects a rectangle.
Move and gather: Choose a command then tap a destination. Workers forage automatically when idle and carry food home through the same entrance you see underground.
Build: When prompted, switch to Nest and tap soil or choose Mark nursery tunnel. Wait while actual workers excavate six cells; spend 45 food to build the nursery.
Warfare: Train soldiers, hold the marked food cache, then defend the entrance when the red raid begins. Attack the outpost and keep ants on it to win.
Camera: Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. +/− and the crosshair control also work. Keyboard: 1 Workers, 2 Soldiers, 3 All; N switches layer; Space pauses.
